Frequently Asked Questions

What are pancreatic digestive enzymes?

Protease, amylase, and lipase are pancreatic digestive enzymes — natural juices created by your pancreas to help break down food.

Why take ZYMPAL?†

A digestive enzyme supplement can ease incidental bloating and indigestion following a heavy meal, and improve digestibility of macronutrients to support their nutritive benefits. Proper digestion of dietary proteins and fat can directly impact performance and overall health.†

How is ZYMPAL different from probiotics?

Probiotics are helpful bacteria living in your gut that can support the work of digestive enzymes, but probiotics are not able to break down food. Digestive enzymes like ZYMPAL can.

How is ZYMPAL different from other digestive enzyme supplements?

Unlike other supplements, ZYMPAL contains microspheres with enteric coating to avoid being broken down by stomach acids. The ZYMPAL Targeted Delivery System was developed with advanced pharmaceutical technology. ZYMPAL is manufactured in Canada.

How do I take ZYMPAL?

Adults over 18: take one capsule immediately before or with a meal, or as directed by a healthcare provider. Consult a provider prior to use if pregnant, breastfeeding, or if you have diabetes, pancreatitis, pancreatic exocrine insufficiency, or cystic fibrosis. Capsules may be opened and granules mixed with soft food — do not crush or chew. Store in a cool, dry place.

Where does the Targeted Delivery System deliver enzymes?

The system transports digestive enzymes into the small intestine, where the majority of chemical digestion occurs and nutrients are absorbed into the bloodstream.
